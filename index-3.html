What is TableTap?

TableTap is a digital menu and order management system for restaurants, cafés and hotels. A customer scans the QR code on the table, picks dishes like pizza, burgers or coffee, adds them to the cart and places the order. They can pay online or at the reception, and rate your service afterwards.
